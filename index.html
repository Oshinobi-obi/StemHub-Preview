Participant / Student

Participant website — https://localhost:7299

What a student, teacher or other participant does: from creating an account to downloading a certificate and asking for help.

1Home page

The public home page lists the activities that are open for registration, with the type, mode, dates and slots left. Anyone can browse without logging in.

2Create an account

The participant fills in the login details, name, the full name to print on certificates, school and position, then clicks Create account.

3Log in

Participants log in here with their email and password. Staff accounts are not accepted on this site.

4Browse activities

Activities can be searched and filtered by type and mode. Closed or completed activities show "Registration closed".

5Activity details

Schedule, mode, venue or meeting link, registration deadline, slots left and the requirements with their qualification rules. The Register button is on the right.

6Register for a training, contest or quiz

The participant chooses a role and uploads one file per requirement, then submits. All required files uploaded means For Verification; otherwise Incomplete.

7Register an NSEF team

For the science fair, the project leader registers the whole team in one form: project details, members, research advisers, observers, contact details and the requirement files.

8After submitting the team

The leader sees the new project code, the team list with every role, the submitted files and the status For Verification.

9My Registrations

Every registration with its status badge, the status timeline, remarks from staff and, for NSEF, the project and team.

10A registration in detail

Status and timeline, the project and team, the submitted files (links open in a new tab), and on the right the attendance, result and awards recorded by staff.

11Incomplete: upload a missing file

When staff mark a registration Incomplete, the participant reads the remarks and uploads the missing or corrected file here. The status then returns to For Verification.

12My Certificates

Only certificates released by staff are listed. Each one downloads as a PDF.

13Profile

The participant's own details, with buttons to edit the profile and change the password.

14Edit profile

The "Full name on certificates" box controls exactly what is printed on future certificates.

15Chat with the chatbot

The chat button opens the assistant. It answers from the FAQ and, when asked about status, lists the participant's own registrations.

16Ask for a Customer Care officer

When the bot cannot answer, the participant clicks Talk to a Customer Care Officer and the panel shows that it is waiting for an officer.

17The officer's reply arrives

The Customer Care officer's reply appears in the same panel, live.

Admin

Staff Portal — https://localhost:7135

What the administrator does: set up activities, verify registrations, record attendance and results, issue certificates, and run reports.

1Staff login

Admin, Trainer and Customer Care all log in on this page. Each role is taken to its own pages.

2Dashboard

Counts of open activities, registrations for verification, escalated inquiries and unreleased certificates, the latest registrations, upcoming activities and the 5th NSEF charts.

3Activities

All activities with their dates, mode, status and registration counts. New activity creates one.

4Activity details

Schedule and venue, requirements, assigned trainers and the number of registrations in each status.

5Edit an activity

Every field of the activity, the assigned trainers, and the requirements that participants must submit.

6NSEF Projects

The research projects (teams) of an NSEF activity with code, title, category, school, region, team size, payment and status. Searchable and filterable.

7A project in detail

Project information, payment, the whole team with each person's role and status, the attachments, and the team decision buttons.

8Confirmation before saving

Every button that saves or changes something asks for confirmation first. Here the Admin is about to mark the whole team Qualified.

9Registrations

Every registration, searchable by name, school or project code and filterable by activity, status and role.

10Verify one registration

The participant's details, the submitted files with Accept and Reject, and the decision: Qualified, Incomplete or Not Qualified with remarks.

11Attendance

For an activity and session date, the qualified participants are listed (grouped by project for NSEF) with Present, Late and Absent buttons and the bulk shortcuts.

12Results and awards

Score, ranking and remarks, and awards. For NSEF they are entered once per project and copied to every student researcher.

13Certificates

Create the certificate records, generate the PDFs, preview them and release them to participants.

14FAQ answers

The questions, keywords and answers the chatbot uses. They can be added, edited or switched off.

15Inquiries

The Admin can view every inquiry and its chat history, read-only.

16Reports

Four reports, each viewable on screen and exportable to Excel and PDF.

17A report on screen

NSEF projects per category and per region with total payments, with the export buttons at the top.

18User accounts

Staff and participant accounts. The Admin creates staff accounts and activates or deactivates any account.

Trainer

Staff Portal — https://localhost:7135

Trainers can only view, and only the activities the Admin assigned to them.

1Trainer dashboard

One card per assigned activity with its type, mode, date, venue and number of qualified participants.

2My Activities

The same activities as a schedule table with the venue and meeting link.

3Activity view

Schedule and venue, the attendance summary per day, and the qualified participants with their attendance (grouped by project for NSEF).

Customer Care

Staff Portal — https://localhost:7135

The Customer Care officer answers the questions the chatbot could not.

1Inquiry queue

Tabs for Escalated, Forwarded to Staff, Resolved and All. A newly escalated inquiry appears at the top without refreshing the page.

2Conversation

The full chat history including the chatbot's answers, the participant's details, a live reply box, and the Forward to Staff and Mark Resolved buttons.

3FAQ reference

The chatbot's answers, read-only, to use while helping a participant.
